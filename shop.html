About R&R Gardening

Founded in 2018, R&R Gardening is Portland's favorite neighborhood nursery. We believe everyone deserves a little green in their life — whether you have a sprawling backyard or a sunny windowsill.

Our team of passionate horticulturists hand-selects every plant that comes through our doors. We focus on healthy, locally-adapted varieties that thrive in the Pacific Northwest climate.
